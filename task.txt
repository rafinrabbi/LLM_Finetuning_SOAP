2. Modeling and Fine-Tuning
2.1 Baseline Model & Fine-Tuning
● Architecture Selection: Select a suitable pre-trained, sequence-to-sequence LLM from the
Hugging Face Hub.
● Fine-Tuning: Fine-tune your chosen model on the given dataset. The primary objective is to train
the model to generate summaries that are
○ Accurate: Factually consistent with the original Bengali chat transcript.
○ Coherent: Well-structured and easy to read.
○ Concise: Significantly shorter than the original dialogue while retaining key medical
information.
